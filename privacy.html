Privacy Policy

Effective: December 2026

Who we are

Maitri Embody Lab is the coaching practice of Rebecca Winter, operated by Rebecca Winter, LPC, LPCC, Coach, PLLC. This policy explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

This website does not use contact forms, cookies, or analytics tracking. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, email address, and message.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts load from Google Fonts. These providers may log basic technical information, such as your IP address, as part of delivering the site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and schedule consultations
  • To provide coaching services you request

We do not sell or share your personal information for marketing purposes.

Confidentiality of coaching

Coaching conversations are kept confidential, except where disclosure is required by law or needed to prevent serious harm. Because coaching is not healthcare, coaching information is not governed by HIPAA.

International visitors

If you contact us from outside the United States, your information will be processed in the United States. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information.

Your choices

To ask about, correct, or delete information you've shared with us, email rebecca@maitriembodylab.com.

Accessibility

We aim to make this website accessible to everyone. If you have trouble using any part of it, please email us and we'll be glad to help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
