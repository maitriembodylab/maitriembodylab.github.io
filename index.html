What is Maitri?

Maitri is a Sanskrit word for unconditional friendliness toward yourself. It's the practice of meeting your experience with warmth and honesty, rather than judgment.

Here, you learn to stay present, respond instead of react, and treat yourself with steady kindness, while still growing toward the life you want.

"...settle down with ourselves, just as we are."Pema Chödrön

About Rebecca

I believe the deepest, most lasting change happens when we treat mind and body as one integrated system, and that you already hold the wisdom you need to grow.

My path began more than two decades ago in bodywork, where I learned how stress and emotion live in the body. That led me to a B.A. in Somatic Psychology from Prescott College and an M.A. in Transpersonal Counseling Psychology from Naropa University.

Today, I bring together somatic awareness, parts work, and mindfulness to help women reconnect with their inner wisdom and move forward with clarity and confidence.

I'm based in Boulder, Colorado, and coach clients online around the world.

Coaching is not therapy

Coaching is a non-clinical, goal-oriented partnership. It is not psychotherapy or mental health treatment, and it does not diagnose or treat any condition. Coaching services are separate from any clinical practice, and no therapist-client relationship is formed.

If you are in crisis, please call or text 988 (in the US), call 911, or contact your local emergency services.
